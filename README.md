## About Me

I am a Deep Learning Engineer and Computer Science graduate specializing in building end-to-end AI pipelines. My core expertise lies in Computer Vision, Medical Image Analysis, and Generative AI, where I focus on transforming complex, unaligned data into highly accurate, automated inference models. I bridge the gap between theoretical machine learning and practical, deployable industry solutions.

- **Background:** BS in Computer Science from PAF-IAST.
- **Deep Learning & Vision:** Specializing in state-of-the-art architectures including SAM 2, SegFormer, and advanced GANs (DCLGAN, Pix2Pix).
- **Data Engineering:** Experienced in complete data lifecycles, pixel-by-pixel image registration, and custom enhancement algorithms.
- **Software Architecture:** Building robust, scalable applications utilizing Flutter (MVVM architecture, SOLID principles) and LLM-integrated systems.
- **Contact:** ahmedraza8616@gmail.com

## Featured Projects

### FBR Insight Chatbot (LLM & RAG)
[![FBR Chatbot](https://github-readme-stats.vercel.app/api/pin/?username=meahmedraza&repo=FBR-Insights-Chatbot&theme=radical&show_owner=true)](https://github.com/meahmedraza/FBR-Insights-Chatbot)

An AI-powered chatbot fine-tuned on Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) datasets, integrating LLaMA models with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). It enables users to query tax regulations, policies, and data insights in natural language. The system demonstrates custom fine-tuning with LoRA adapters, precise data preprocessing, and real-time response generation.
